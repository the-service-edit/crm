1 · Start

Keep it quick

“Hey, is that Jimmy? It's Mel from The Service Edit. I'll keep it quick. I sent you some Christmas emails on Sunday and just wanted to hear what you thought?”

Not him: “Oh hey, is Jimmy or Alasdair about? It's Mel, I emailed them on the weekend.”
Flat out: “No stress, when's better for you?” Put the time in step 6.

2 · What did they think

Ask, then stay quiet

“Did you get a chance to look? What'd you reckon?” Count to three before you speak again.

Haven't looked: “All good. Basically, I signed up for the 10% off and nothing came through. Mailchimp's grabbing everyone's emails, it's just not sending them anything. So I made you a welcome email and a couple for Christmas. Have a scroll when you get a minute.”

His words. Which one he liked, anything he'd change.
“What's bigger for you at Christmas, bottles or the experience stuff, like tours and vouchers?”
3 · Before Christmas?

Would they look at it

“Is it something you'd be keen to look at before Christmas?”

4 · Their list

Where the people are

“Out of curiosity, roughly how many people are on your list? With the popup and your NowBookIt bookings?”

“And have you got TalkBox hooked up to NowBookIt, or is it just Mailchimp at the moment?”

TalkBox: “Oh good, I work in TalkBox already, so I can send it all from there.”
Just Mailchimp: “So your booking guests and newsletter people are in two places. Part of what I'd do is get them working together.” Don't sell TalkBox today.
Not sure: “No stress, I can check it with you once we get going.”

The results month needs about 1,000.
What they send from.
“Do your NowBookIt bookings have the marketing tick box turned on?” That's usually most of the list.
Shop customers, Veterans Club, shareholders, who sends now.
5 · The offer

Say it, then stop

About 1,000 or more: “Okay, so here's what I do. First month, I set it all up and send these out before Christmas. If they don't make you more than my fee, which is $750, you just don't pay me. No contract either.”

“So honestly, there's no money risk for you. It either pays for itself or it's free.”

Under 1,000 or not sure: “That's fine. I'd probably just start with the welcome and getting more people on the list from your bookings. I'll pop it all in an email so you can have a look.”

Say “no money risk”, never “no risk at all”.
